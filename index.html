In Loving Memory

Laurie Torrence, my beloved wife of nearly 38 years, passed away on August 23, 2024. We were married on August 16, 1986, and she dedicated her life to education as a teacher, touching countless young lives. Her memory lives on through our family and the many students she inspired throughout her career.
